# NULL SIGNAL — concise continuity bible

The established story in GAME_STORY.md remains authoritative. This document records the Phase 7 staging choices; it does not add a new gameplay chapter.

In 2189, the Indian-led Astra-7 station operates the Anveshak research array. Ananya Rao leads the experiment; Lubna monitors the search. They repeat a controlled operation on a weak signal until an unexpected presence answers. The station fails. Seventy-two hours later, retrieval operative and former engineer Anirudh Varma arrives to recover the Core and shut the Array down. TARA is his practical guide; ANVESH is his search interface.

The opening shows cause and reaction: a research action, changing amplitudes, the impossible reply, the scientists stepping back, emergency systems, a fragmented humanoid forming, and Anirudh entering the damaged dock. It leaves the Vault recording to explain what the researchers amplified. The stylized station collapse and distant signatures are science-fiction consequences, not claims about physical quantum computers.

Ananya is decisive until the display answers. Lubna is observant and the first to recoil. Anirudh's movement and short mission line establish resolve. TARA stays concise. The unknown voice remains restrained rather than theatrical. No final voice performance is claimed when only subtitle/audio slots are available.

After extraction, quiet briefly suggests safety. The inactive interface reactivates. Anirudh questions it; the unknown presence answers. Multiple distant structures appear without a new lecture or gameplay task. The implication is that the search can operate in both directions.

## Production limits

The actors are procedural articulated geometry with skin, clothing, glasses/hair and continuously evaluated gestures, not motion-captured or lip-synced production characters. No raster stills, prerendered video, external model pack, or new package is required. The human final smoke test must judge camera blocking, motion and readability; static compilation cannot establish those qualities.
